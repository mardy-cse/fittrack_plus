### Q: Why aren't my stats showing on the Progress tab?
**A:** Check these in order:
1. [VISUAL_WORKFLOW_DIAGRAMS.md](VISUAL_WORKFLOW_DIAGRAMS.md) - Diagram 9 (Troubleshooting)
2. [APP_WORKFLOW_DOCUMENTATION.md](APP_WORKFLOW_DOCUMENTATION.md) - "When Progress Stats Don't Update"

- **Progress**: All-time totals
