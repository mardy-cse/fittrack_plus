## 💡 Pro Tips

### File Size
- ✅ Good: Under 100KB
- ⚠️ OK: 100-500KB
- ❌ Too large: Over 500KB

### Format
- `.lottie` (dotLottie) - Better, compressed
- `.json` - Standard, works everywhere

### Quality Check
Look for:
- ✅ Smooth looping
- ✅ Clear movements
- ✅ Minimal/flat design
- ✅ 2-4 second duration
